Anatomy — refined front, v2

One continuous contour per body: torso, arm and leg are a single outline with a real armpit, a tapering waist and a hip flare, instead of stacked limb blobs. Bellies follow fibre direction — the pec fans off the sternum with the clavicular head above it, serratus interdigitates beneath it, the vastus lateralis sweeps out while the medialis drops to the knee. Curves are splined through plotted landmarks, not eyeballed.
